\clearpage\section{State of the art of the technology used or applied
in this thesis:}

 With the boom of AI that is currently on-going, the offer of
 automated tools to generate hardware has increased greatly. These
 tools could be broken down into two main groups. The ones based on
 Large Language Models (LLM) and the ones that use other techniques.

 In the first one, several works based on LLM HDL code generation can
 be found. Some examples are DeepRTL \cite{deeprtl} o RTLCoder
 \cite{liu2025rtlcoder}. The second group includes other techniques
 that do not use LLMs, such as \cite{autoai2c}.

 On the architectural exploration side, there are also some other
 utilites such as ZigZag\cite{zigzag}.
